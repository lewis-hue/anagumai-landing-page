How we work

  1. Align and Scope
  2. Build in the Open
  3. Launch and Own

Align and Scope

Honest technical discovery before a single line of code is billed. If AI is not the right tool, or we are not the right partner, we tell you upfront.

You leave with: written architecture scope, a realistic timeline, and a fixed milestone estimate.

Build in the Open

Short weekly shipping cycles into a live staging environment, so your team tests real workflows early. Not slide decks.

You leave with: weekly working builds, staging access, and fast feedback loops.

Launch and Own

Production deployment, tuning against real edge cases, and complete handover so your team runs the system independently.

You leave with: 100% IP ownership, a clean documented codebase, runbooks, and zero lock-in.
