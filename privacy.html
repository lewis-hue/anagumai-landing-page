Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026. Anagum AI collects as little as possible, keeps what it collects inside scoped systems, and deletes on request.

1. Website inquiries

When you contact us through the project form, scoping-call links, or direct email, we receive the details you send: your name, email address, company, and the content of your message. We use this only to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the engagement. Project inquiries go to sales@anagumai.com; product support goes to support@anagumai.com. We do not sell contact data or add you to marketing lists.

2. Job applications

Applications submitted on our careers page include your name, email, role, links, cover note, and resume. Applications are processed through our form provider (Basin) and reviewed by our hiring team. We keep applications for up to 12 months so we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

3. Website technology

This site is plain static files: no accounts, no tracking scripts, and no third-party analytics. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives standard request data (IP address, user agent) under Google's own privacy policy. Our server keeps standard operational logs for security and reliability, retained for up to 90 days.

4. Client project data

Data you share during a client engagement (documents, credentials, repository access, customer data) is handled under your contract and any NDA in force. It stays inside scoped systems with role-based access, is used only to deliver your project, and is returned or deleted at handover on your instruction. Client data is never used to train models and never shared with other clients.

5. THE EYE product data

THE EYE (app.anagumai.com) is our in-house research worksuite. When you use it, we process:

  • Account data: your name, email address, and sign-in identity, used to provide your account and workspace access.
  • Workspace content: the scans, documents, messages, files, events, and finance entries you create, stored so the product works and scoped so only your workspace members can see them.
  • Operational data: audit logs, usage metering, and error logs used for security, reliability, and billing.

AI features send the content you choose to process to model and search providers strictly to generate your results; workspace content is never used to train models. Recordings and transcripts you create stay inside your workspace with the same scoping. Data is hosted with reputable cloud infrastructure providers under their audited controls.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time, including full deletion of your THE EYE account and workspace content. Write to support@anagumai.com from the address concerned and we will respond within 30 days. Deletion removes your data from production systems; limited records may remain in backups for up to 90 days before they age out.

7. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes materially, we update the effective date above and, for product changes, notify account holders. Continued use after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@anagumai.com. Company details: About Anagum AI.
