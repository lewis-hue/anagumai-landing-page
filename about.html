About Anagum AI

Anagum AI is an AI agency for companies that want working systems, not demos. We design and build AI automations, AI agents, web platforms and mobile apps — then we stay to care for them in production.

What we believe

Production over demos

A prototype that impresses once and breaks twice is not a solution. We build for daily use: citation-checked outputs, background jobs, permissions and human review where it matters.

Honest scoping

Discovery comes before billing. If the wrong tool, the wrong timing or the wrong partner is staring at us, we say so upfront — even when it costs us the project.

Ownership stays with you

You keep 100% of the IP, the repositories, the runbooks and the data. Clean code you can inspect, hand over or extend without us. Zero lock-in.

Engineering standards on every project

The same bar for client work and our own products. Every system we ship respects data boundaries, keeps a human in the loop for consequential decisions, and can explain what it did.
