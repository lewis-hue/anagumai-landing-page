Careers at Anagum AI

We are a small, remote-first team building AI systems people rely on every day. If you like shipping to production and owning your work, you will fit right in.

See open roles Open application

How we work

Remote-first and async

Work from where you do your best work. Written updates over meetings, overlap hours for pairing, and trust by default.

Small senior team

Few people, big ownership. You will talk to clients, shape scope and see your work run in production within weeks.

Production bar

Citation-checked outputs, tests that matter, clean handoffs. We review each other's work and keep the bar high together.

Open roles

Three openings on a small team. Every role is remote.

Remote · Full-time

Senior AI Engineer

Design and ship LLM systems for clients: knowledge copilots, extraction pipelines and AI agents running in production.

  • You have shipped production features built on LLMs, not just prototypes.
  • You are comfortable with retrieval, citations and evals that prove outputs hold.
  • You write Python and TypeScript, and you review code with care.

Apply for this role

Remote · Full-time

Full-Stack Engineer

Build customer portals, dashboards and AI-native SaaS: Next.js frontends, clean APIs and Postgres backends with auth and permissions.

  • You have shipped production Next.js apps backed by Postgres.
  • You design APIs that stay boring in the best way: predictable and documented.
  • You care about the last 10 percent of fit and finish in UI.

Apply for this role

Remote · Contract

Product Designer (Contract)

Turn complex workflows into clear interfaces for portals and dashboards. Own design end to end with engineers, from first sketch to shipped UI.

  • Your portfolio shows B2B or SaaS work used by real teams.
  • You think in systems: components, states and edge cases included.
  • You collaborate async and defend decisions with user reasoning.
